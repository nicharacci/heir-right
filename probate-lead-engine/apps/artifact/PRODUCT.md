# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HeirRight real estate operators reviewing probate and heirship opportunities. They know the deal process, but should not need software or developer knowledge to understand an estate's disposition, inspect evidence, upload an IDI Core report, or decide the next action.

## Product Purpose

HeirRight turns a selected estate file and operator-supplied evidence into a reviewable Discovery packet. Success means an operator can begin on Dashboard, understand whether to move forward, review, block, or move on, upload a PDF or DOCX IDI report against the exact estate, watch verified extraction and automated Discovery complete, inspect the active packet, and use optional Google Workspace or ChatGPT Work handoffs without weakening human review, source provenance, or stop rules.

## Brand Personality

Assured, editorial, operational. The app should feel like a focused estate workbench: graphite and cream surfaces, iOS/HeirRight blue interaction emphasis in both themes, clear status language, dense but comfortable information, and purposeful motion that explains state.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue-purple SaaS gradients, card grids, and developer-console language; HeirRight blue is a disciplined interaction color, not a decorative wash.
- Decorative glass, glow halos, spinner ornaments, icon tiles, and boxed ordinary controls.
- Eye-icon document actions, row-local action clutter, detached drawers, duplicated rails, or hidden next steps.
- Fintheon's trading colors or semantics; its shell and rail architecture are reference-only.

## Design Principles

- Make disposition, current lifecycle stage, and next operator action immediately legible.
- Preserve evidence truth, stop conditions, exact-estate binding, and human review at every handoff.
- Use one continuous shell, one contextual rail, and whole-row document interactions so the interface follows the operator's case journey.
- Keep actions obvious and functional while leaving ordinary navigation, rows, and metadata visually quiet.
- Treat local packet completion as the dependable outcome; optional providers add handoff value without blocking it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, provide complete pointer and keyboard operation, maintain 44px touch targets, support reduced motion, avoid color-only status meaning, and keep mobile sheets and overlays free of clipped content or trapped focus.
