About Us

Who We Are

We are a family-owned business proudly rooted in the heart of South Florida and Texas. Over eight years ago, Joshua began his real estate journey in Houston, Texas, where he successfully bought, rehabbed, and sold numerous residential properties. His work expanded into land development and, over time, he developed a strong niche in helping clients navigate the often complicated world of inherited real estate.

Whether it was residential, land, or commercial property, Joshua and his team became known for providing personalized solutions to heirs dealing with complex estate and title issues. After building a historic track record in the Texas market, Joshua and his family relocated to Florida with a mission: help families liquidate their inheritance so they could enjoy the sunshine state they once envisioned.

At HeirRight, our goal is to provide heirs with a seamless and professional experience during what can be a challenging time. With extensive knowledge of the probate processes, clearing liens or resolving disputes with co-heirs or creditors, we've seen it all - and handled it with care and effectiveness. Our team offers quick, fair cash offers without the stress of dealing with repairs, mortgages, or attorney fees.

Joshua spearheads every transaction personally, ensuring each heir receives a custom-tailored solution. At HeirRight, we carry the heavy load so you don't have to - and we're confident we will resolve your inherited property with success.
